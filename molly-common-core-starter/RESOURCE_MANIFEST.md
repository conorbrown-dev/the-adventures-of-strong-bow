# Curriculum Resource and Licensing Manifest

Verified for planning on July 24, 2026. This is an engineering inventory, not legal
advice. Preserve item-level notices and re-check licenses before a commercial release.

## 1. Common Core State Standards — official source of truth

### Official machine-readable package

- URL: https://thecorestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/ccssi.zip
- Purpose: import official identifiers, statements, hierarchy, and metadata
- License: Common Core Public License
- Shipping policy: allowed when the license is followed
- Required notice:
  `© Copyright 2010. National Governors Association Center for Best Practices and
  Council of Chief State School Officers. All rights reserved.`
- Important limitation: the public license covers the standards, not every example
  or third-party excerpt appearing in related materials.

### Mathematics PDF

- URL: https://corestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Math_Standards1.pdf

## 2. Illustrative Mathematics K–5

- URL: https://im.kendallhunt.com/k5/curriculum.html
- License: CC BY 4.0 for the curriculum materials except excluded marks and
  separately licensed content
- Best use:
  - mathematical progression;
  - lesson and assessment design patterns;
  - misconception research;
  - inspiration for original parameterized templates.
- Do not copy:
  - Illustrative Mathematics name/logo as branding;
  - images without checking their individual attribution;
  - content without preserving required attribution.
- Recommended shipping policy: prefer original Molly's Learning Game wording and
  graphics while recording IM as design inspiration when a template is adapted.

## 3. Achieve the Core

- Permissions URL: https://achievethecore.org/ccpd
- General permission shown there: CC0/public-domain dedication
- Best use:
  - assessment structure;
  - public-domain item patterns;
  - grade-level rigor examples.
- Caution: individual pages can contain third-party or separately licensed material.
  Preserve the exact source page and its notices for anything copied or adapted.
- Recommended shipping policy: do not bulk scrape. Import only deliberately selected
  items whose page-level provenance has been reviewed.

## 4. EL Education K–5 Language Arts, 2017 edition

- Curriculum URL: https://access.openupresources.org/curricula/el-k5-2017
- Licensing URL: https://www.openupresources.org/help-support/licensing-questions/
- License: generally CC BY 4.0 for the 2017 edition, except identified third-party
  content
- Best use:
  - ELA progression;
  - foundational-skill sequencing;
  - lesson objectives;
  - assessment and discussion structures.
- Do not copy:
  - third-party stories, poems, book excerpts, artwork, or photographs without
    separate permission;
  - Open Up Resources or EL Education branding as game branding.
- Recommended shipping policy: create original passages and graphics. Adapt only
  clearly CC BY material and retain attribution/provenance.

## 5. EL Education newer editions

- 2024 K–2 Skills Block and 2025 K–5 Language Arts are generally CC BY-NC 4.0.
- Do not ingest them into a potentially commercial game.
- They may be read for private evaluation, but do not copy/adapt their protected
  content into the product without a suitable commercial license.

## 6. Smarter Balanced samples

- URL: https://smarterbalanced.org/our-system/students-and-families/samples/

- Shipping policy: reference-only until the rights for a specific item are verified.
  Do not copy sample items into the question bank by default.

## Provenance policy

Every non-original template or passage must record:

- source title;
- source URL;
- original author/organization;
- license;
- whether it is copied, adapted, or merely inspired;
- attribution text;
- date reviewed;
- reviewer;
- excluded third-party components removed or replaced.
