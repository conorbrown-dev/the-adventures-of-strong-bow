# Ticket 4: Kindergarten–Grade 2 Vertical Slice

Create an original, reviewed starter content bank covering the highest-priority
K–2 reading-foundational and mathematics skills. Use templates and generators,
not thousands of copied static questions.

Prioritize:

- letter recognition and letter-sound correspondence;
- vowels versus consonants;
- rhyming, syllables, blending, and CVC segmentation;
- decoding common one-syllable words;
- counting, cardinality, number comparison, and number writing;
- addition and subtraction foundations;
- place value and arithmetic within Grade 2 expectations;
- shapes, measurement comparisons, time, money, and simple data displays.

Acceptance criteria:

- every template maps to an imported official standard;
- every item is original or has explicit reusable provenance;
- low-reading tasks have audio-capable prompts;
- distractors model realistic misconceptions;
- each covered standard has more than one item form where feasible;
- coverage report clearly identifies remaining K–2 gaps.
