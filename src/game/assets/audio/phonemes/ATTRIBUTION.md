# Provisional phoneme audio attribution

These files are unmodified copies downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on
2026-08-31. They are available under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
and, as described on each source page, the GNU Free Documentation License.

They are general IPA samples used as a private-preview bridge. They have not
been reviewed as isolated U.S. Kindergarten phonics recordings. In particular,
some samples contain surrounding vowel context. Do not mark them `REVIEWED` or
use them for a public curriculum-readiness claim without qualified listening
review and real-device testing.
