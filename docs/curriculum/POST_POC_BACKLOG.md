# Post-POC backlog

These items are explicitly deferred and were not implemented during post-approval verification.

## Instructional progression and generalization

- Familiar-word introduction and controlled repetition.
- Gradual word-family introduction and mixed-family practice.
- Previously unseen and, where educationally appropriate, nonsense-word transfer items.
- Recognition-to-production progression.
- Exposure tracking by word and family, including reduced frequency after overexposure.
- Mastery evidence requiring generalization rather than memorized pairs.

## Broader curriculum expansion

- Complete Kindergarten coverage.
- Grade 1, Grade 2, and Grades 3–5 content.
- Parent assignment tools, reporting, and adult review of constructed responses.
- Optional future game integration, only after standalone Learning is stable and separately approved.
