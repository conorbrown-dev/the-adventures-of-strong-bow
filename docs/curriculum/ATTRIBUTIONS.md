# Attribution Notices

## Common Core State Standards

Include this notice in repository documentation and any public standards browser:

> © Copyright 2010. National Governors Association Center for Best Practices and
> Council of Chief State School Officers. All rights reserved.

Also state:

> Molly's Learning Game is an independent product and is not endorsed by the
> National Governors Association Center for Best Practices or the Council of Chief
> State School Officers.

Do not imply that child-friendly paraphrases are official standards. Label the exact
statement and paraphrase separately.

## Illustrative Mathematics adaptations

When a shipped item is actually adapted from IM K–5 rather than merely informed by
general teaching ideas, retain a record substantially equivalent to:

> Adapted from IM K–5 Math, © 2021 Illustrative Mathematics, licensed under
> CC BY 4.0. Changes were made. Illustrative Mathematics names and logos are not
> used as product branding.

Link to the exact lesson/item in item-level provenance when possible.

## EL Education 2017 adaptations

When a shipped item is adapted from curriculum content that is confirmed to be
covered by CC BY 4.0, retain an item-level attribution that names EL Education and
links to the source and license. Do not treat third-party texts or images as covered
by the curriculum's general license.

## CC0 material

Attribution may not be legally required for verified CC0 material, but the project
should still retain source provenance for auditability and educational review.
